Story

More photos

I've been starting things since 2017, when I led a team to win Techstars Startup Weekend Trichy with The Cocoon, a shade that mounts on a bicycle.

Then I went inside companies to learn how things ship. At Lal PathLabs I built automation that cut instrument downtime 15% and technician workload 90%. I also told the CEO I could plan a sample-sorting bulk loader in-house, and delivered the plan within a month. At Thorogood I led one of four workstreams on a finance platform for a Fortune 500 company and built anomaly detection on 30+ KPIs.

At UMass Amherst I studied decision analytics and went back to building from zero. Sweeesh won NYU's Trust in the Hiring Funnel track, and Bookworm took 3rd at the Viam hackathon. Other builds include an agent that learns which evidence to check, an ML tool that runs entirely in the browser, an oil-field toolkit study, and a phone case that deploys dampers when dropped.

It all comes from controls engineering at NIT Trichy: measure the system, notice when it drifts, correct it. I want to build companies on that idea, with software and machines that notice what's wrong and act on it.

Based in NYC. I care about sustainability, fairness and responsible AI.

The work

  1. The CocoonProduct design & team lead

    A modular shade that mounts on a bicycle, so students can ride across campus in sun or rain. I led the team: we designed it in CAD, surveyed students on demand, and planned a two-tier launch in canvas and nylon. Winner, Techstars Startup Weekend Trichy 2017.

    Team leadership, product design, CAD, market research, pitching

  2. Lab Instrument AutomationLal PathLabs

    An end-to-end automation product in C#/.NET for a diagnostics network serving 26.7M patients a year. It cut global instrument downtime by 15%, and the interface automation reduced technician workload by 90%.

    C#, .NET, SQL, Python

  3. Sample-Sorting Bulk LoaderLal PathLabs

    I challenged the CEO of Lal PathLabs, saying I could plan this machine in-house, and delivered the plan within a month. A project plan and design study for a machine that reads each sample tube's barcode and cap color and sorts it into the right bin. Scoped customer needs and throughput targets, specified the sensors and controls, and designed two CAD concepts, linear and rotary.

    Project planning, product development, CAD, embedded systemscode

  4. Audit Control AnalyticsThorogood

    A finance controls platform for a Fortune 500 FMCG company. I led one of its four streams, building pipelines across 20+ sources in Databricks, Azure Data Factory and Delta Lake to surface data quality issues and operational risk. Two automations I built cut the monthly refresh time by 35%.

    Databricks, Azure Data Factory, Delta Lake, Azure Analysis Services

  5. KPI Anomaly DetectionThorogood

    Automated anomaly detection across 30+ business KPIs, built on Snowflake and dbt with statistical scoring. Alerts and NLP-generated insight summaries reached teams across the business.

    Snowflake, dbt, statistics, NLP

  6. SweeeshTrust systems & cryptography

    Resumes where every claim can be verified without a phone call, because each one is signed up a chain of trust to a root registry. I built and deployed the verifiable-credential demo, and pitched it at the Minute Pitch startup competition at UMass Amherst. Winner, Trust in the Hiring Funnel track, NYU.

    JavaScript, Python, verifiable credentials, cryptographycodelive

  7. BookwormComputer vision & closed-loop control

    A robot arm that turns real book pages, then reads them aloud or scans them into a searchable PDF without damaging the book. I built the calibration, the touch-based book model and the adaptive page-flip path. 3rd place, Viam "Code that Moves" Hackathon.

    Python, robotics, Viam, computer vision, OCRcode

  8. RobologueAI agents · demo

    An AI agent that remembers unresolved mistakes across a long task and learns which evidence to check before marking a step done, with memory stored in MongoDB Atlas.

    Python, AI agents, MongoDB Atlas, evaluationcodelive

  9. Tabular AutopilotML product · demo

    Drop in a CSV and get a full analysis, from cleaning and feature engineering to model comparison and diagnostics. The Python engine runs entirely in the browser via WebAssembly, so the data never leaves your machine. Best results: R² 0.825 on California Housing and 0.838 ROC-AUC on Titanic.

    Python, scikit-learn, WebAssembly, Pyodidecodelive

  10. OnSite ToolkitProject management & planning

    A project management study for a PDO (Petroleum Development Oman) competition: scoping, planning and prototyping a phone toolkit for oil-field technicians. It pulls tables and images out of 40-page equipment datasheets into a zone, process, equipment and component hierarchy, so a technician can pin just the values they need to a personal dashboard, with safety checklists and warnings alongside.

    Project planning, table extraction, dashboards, mobile, oil & gas

  11. Passive Free-Fall DetectorMechatronics & IP

    A phone case that deploys dampers when dropped, triggered by an unpowered mechanical 3-axis switch instead of an accelerometer and microcontroller. Drop tests with a phone's accelerometer set the targets: release within 0.5 s, with the sensor at the center of mass.

    CAD, sensors, mechatronics, patent reviewcode
