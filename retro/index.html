01Story

I've been starting things since 2017. That year I led a team to win Techstars Startup Weekend Trichy with The Cocoon, a modular shade that mounts on a bicycle. We designed it in CAD, surveyed students to test demand, and pitched a two-tier launch.

Then I went inside companies to learn how things actually get shipped. At Lal PathLabs I built the automation product that cut instrument downtime 15% and technician workload 90% across a diagnostics network serving 26.7 million patients a year, and I told the CEO I could plan a sample-sorting bulk loader in-house, then delivered the plan within a month. At Thorogood I led one of four workstreams on a finance controls platform for a Fortune 500 company and built anomaly detection on 30+ KPIs. Both jobs taught me the same thing: the value is in the decision, not the dashboard.

At UMass Amherst I studied decision analytics and went back to building from zero. I pitched Sweeesh at the Minute Pitch startup competition and won the NYU Trust in the Hiring Funnel track with it. Bookworm took 3rd at the Viam "Code that Moves" hackathon. Robologue and Tabular Autopilot are working demos you can try.

The through-line starts with controls engineering at NIT Trichy, where everything is a feedback loop: measure the system, notice when it drifts, correct it. I want to build companies on that idea: software and machines that notice what's wrong and act on it, instead of waiting for a person to look.

Based in NYC. I care about sustainability, fairness and responsible AI.
