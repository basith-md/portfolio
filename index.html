About Me

I'm an analytics engineer. For three years I've built the data platforms and detection systems that tell teams when something is off: finance controls for a Fortune 500 FMCG company, anomaly detection on 30+ business KPIs, and instrument automation that cut downtime 15% across a diagnostics network serving 26.7 million patients a year.

My master's in decision analytics at UMass Amherst added the step most systems skip: turning what the data says into a decision someone can act on. Now I'm building AI that closes that gap, from agents that check their evidence before acting to a robot arm that adjusts to every book.

It started with controls engineering at NIT Trichy, where everything is a feedback loop: measure the system, notice when it drifts, correct it. That's still how I approach data, and it's how I first worked with real sensor data, modeling rover telemetry at SenseGrass.

Based in NYC and open to analytics and AI roles. Outside work, I care about sustainability, fairness and responsible AI.
